Her Story

We never got to meet our grandmother Lois. She passed away when our dad was young, but her story didn't stop there. It kept living, passed down through everyone who knew her.

As Head Nurse of the Emergency Department at Jackson Memorial Hospital, Lois spent her career steady in the middle of chaos, guiding patients and staff alike through some of the hardest moments of their lives.

But ask anyone who knew her, and they won't just talk about her title. They'll talk about her warmth. Her laugh. The way she made people feel seen the moment she walked into a room and how, even though she carried herself with a beauty and grace that could feel a little intimidating at first, she never once made you feel small. She made you feel inspired.

Lois gave her life away in every direction. To her patients in the ER. To her church family. To the kids at her local Boys & Girls Club. To the young nurses just starting out, she took the time to mentor and encourage, because she remembered what it felt like to be new and unsure.

We may never have known her voice or her laugh ourselves, but we’ve inherited her legacy — and we want to carry it forward. The Lois Ellen Gibbs-McCullar was created to do just that: to help the next generation of Florida nursing students become the kind of nurse she was — skilled, steady, and full of love. Every gift, every scholarship awarded, is one more way her legacy keeps showing up for people, just like she always did.

The LEGM Scholarship Application

Supporting Florida nursing students who lead with compassion and service.

Application Requirements

  • Start Date: November 1, 2026
  • Deadline: December 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM EST
  • Personal Essay (300–500 words): Why are you pursuing nursing and how have you served your community?
  • Letter of Recommendation: Optional but encouraged.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Enrolled or accepted in an accredited Florida nursing program (ADN, BSN, MSN, or Accelerated).
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale.
  • Actively involved in community volunteer work or service programs.
  • Open to domestic and international students studying in Florida.
