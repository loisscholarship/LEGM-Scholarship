Her Story

We’ve never got a chance to meet our grandmother Lois. Our father was young when she passed. However, our family shared the many remarkable stories of how she dedicated her life to serve her cummunity.

As Head Nurse of the Emergency Department at Jackson Memorial Hospital. Lois spent her career poised in the middle of chaos. Guiding patients and staff alike through some of the hardest moments of their lives. She made critical decisions when they mattered the most.

When you ask anyone that witnessed her grace, they won't just talk about her title. They'll talk about her warmth, laugh and how she made people feel. When she walked into a room — and how she carried herself. With beauty and grace, she never once made you feel small. She made you feel inspired to conquer the world.

Lois gave it her all in every way. To her family, the patients she cared for in the ER. Her church family that help to infuse her faith and her dedication to service. To the kids at the local Boys & Girls Club. To the young nurses just starting out, she took the time to mentor and encourage the future of health care.

Although we may never know how her voice or her laugh sounds. The blessing is that we inherited her legacy and spirit. We will carry her legacy and spirit forward. The Lois Ellen Gibbs-McCullar Scholarship was created to do just that. To help the next generation of Florida nursing students become the kind of nurses she was. Skilled, poise, and full of a kind heart. Every gift, every scholarship is one way to continue her legacy. By showing up for people, just like she always did for her community.

The LEGM Scholarship Application

Supporting Florida nursing students who lead with compassion and service.

Application Requirements

  • Start Date: November 1, 2026
  • Deadline: December 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM EST
  • Personal Essay (300–500 words): Why are you pursuing nursing and how have you served your community?
  • Letter of Recommendation: Optional but encouraged.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Enrolled or accepted in an accredited Florida nursing program (ADN, BSN, MSN, or Accelerated).
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale.
  • Actively involved in community volunteer work or service programs.
  • Open to domestic and international students studying in Florida.
