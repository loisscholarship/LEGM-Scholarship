Privacy Policy

The Lois Ellen Gibbs-McCullar Scholarship is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our scholarship applicants, donors, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, and protect your information when you visit our website or submit an application.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when applying for a scholarship or contacting our team:

Applicant Data: Full name, email address, phone number, mailing address, educational background (school, GPA, academic records), and personal essay responses.

Donor & Supporter Information: Basic contact details provided when navigating to our third-party donation partner (Givebutter).

Communication Data: Information provided when you email or message us at loismccullarscholarship@gmail.com.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively for legitimate organizational purposes, including:

Processing, reviewing, and evaluating scholarship applications.

Communicating with applicants regarding application status, awards, and follow-up requirements.

Acknowledging donations and sending organizational updates to supporters.

Maintaining site security and improving user experience.

Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No Selling or Renting: We strictly do not sell, rent, trade, or lease personal information to third parties or marketing companies under any circumstances.

Third-Party Service Providers: We may share necessary data with trusted third-party providers (such as web hosting services or our donation platform, Givebutter) solely to enable site functionality and process operations securely.

Data Storage: Personal application details are stored securely and accessed only by authorized members of The Lois Scholarship selection committee.

Applicant & User Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your application data once the scholarship selection cycle is complete. To make a request, please contact us at loismccullarscholarship@gmail.com.
